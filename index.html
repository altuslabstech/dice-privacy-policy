This Privacy Policy explains how Dice Roller - Simple & Random, developed by Altus Labs, handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Dice Roller - Simple & Random does not require users to create an account, sign in, or directly provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, or other personal details.

Altus Labs does not operate a backend server for collecting personal information from users of this app.

However, the app uses third-party services, including Google AdMob, which may automatically collect and share certain information as described below.

2. Advertising and Google AdMob

Dice Roller - Simple & Random displays advertisements provided by Google AdMob.

Through the Google Mobile Ads SDK, Google may automatically collect and share certain information, including:

  • Approximate location, which may be estimated from the device's IP address.
  • App interactions, such as app launches, taps, and interactions with advertisements.
  • Diagnostic information, such as information related to app and advertising SDK performance.
  • Device or other identifiers, including identifiers such as the Android Advertising ID and App Set ID, where applicable.

This information may be used by Google for purposes including:

  • Advertising and measuring advertising performance.
  • Analytics.
  • Fraud prevention, security, and compliance.

Altus Labs does not directly maintain a database containing this information. Google processes information collected through its services according to Google's own privacy policies and applicable terms.

You can learn more about how Google handles data here: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

3. Permissions and Device Access

Dice Roller - Simple & Random may use the device's vibration functionality solely to provide haptic feedback when rolling dice.

The app also uses internet and network access to load and display advertisements through Google AdMob.

Dice Roller - Simple & Random does not request precise GPS location access for its core functionality. However, approximate location may be estimated by Google AdMob using information such as the device's IP address.

4. Local Data Storage

App settings such as sound, vibration, theme, and display preferences may be stored locally on your device.

Altus Labs does not transmit these locally stored app preferences to its own servers.

Local app data normally remains on the device until the user clears the app's data or uninstalls the application.

5. Data Sharing

Altus Labs does not sell users' personal information.

Data may be transmitted to and processed by Google through the Google Mobile Ads SDK for advertising, analytics, fraud prevention, security, and compliance purposes.

Data handled by Google is subject to Google's Privacy Policy and applicable Google service terms.

6. Data Security

Data transmitted by the Google Mobile Ads SDK is protected in transit using secure encrypted connections.

App preferences stored locally by Dice Roller - Simple & Random remain on the user's device and are not transmitted to Altus Labs servers.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Altus Labs does not maintain user accounts or a server-side database containing personal information collected from users of Dice Roller - Simple & Random.

Locally stored app settings can be removed by clearing the app's storage or data through Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Information collected and processed by Google AdMob is retained and deleted according to Google's applicable data retention policies and privacy controls.

Users may also use Android and Google privacy controls to manage certain advertising identifiers and advertising preferences.

8. Children's Privacy

Dice Roller - Simple & Random does not require user accounts, and Altus Labs does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children through its own servers.

Information processed by third-party services such as Google AdMob is handled according to the policies and requirements applicable to those services.

9. Third-Party Services

Dice Roller - Simple & Random uses Google AdMob for advertising. Google may collect, process, and share information as described in this Privacy Policy and in Google's own privacy documentation.

Please review Google's Privacy Policy for additional information about Google's data practices and privacy controls: https://policies.google.com/privacy .

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the app, its features, third-party services, or applicable privacy requirements change.

Any updates will be published on this page together with a revised Last updated date.
